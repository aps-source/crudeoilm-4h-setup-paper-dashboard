Setups

A row is one 4H sweep of a swing low (long) or high (short) that closed back. No FVG: no fair value gap formed within 6 4H candles. Watching gap: a gap formed and the trader waits for a 15m confirmation candle in it (a Telegram "setup ARMED" alert goes out). Cancelled: a 15m candle closed through the far side of the gap. Expired: 12 4H candles passed without an entry. Skipped: a valid entry while another trade was open (one position at a time). Entry: traded. Live rows cover the last 90 days; rows before the paper start are marked "pre-paper".
Live rows come from signal_log.json (updated by the paper trader); backtest-period rows from backtest_data.json. Read-only page.
