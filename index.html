Strategy rules

1. A 4H candle trades through the last 4H swing low (high) and closes back above (below) it: the liquidity sweep. 2. Within 6 4H candles a bullish (bearish) fair value gap forms: candle 1's high below candle 3's low. 3. On the 15m chart, wait for price to come back into the gap, then enter at the close of the first candle closing in the trade's direction. The setup is cancelled by a 15m close through the far side of the gap or after 12 4H candles. 4. Stop 1 tick beyond the gap or the touch extreme, target 3 × risk. One position at a time, no trend or CPR filter. Costs about 7 points per round trip (brokerage, CTT, exchange, 1 tick slippage per side). No orders are placed.

Reads live_state.json every 60 s. The paper trader checks after every 15-minute candle (MCX 09:00–23:30/23:55 IST) and pushes on every entry, exit and end-of-day summary, otherwise every 10 minutes. Entry and exit alerts with 4K charts go to the project's Telegram channel. Read-only page.
